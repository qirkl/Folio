Bordeaux is a port city in southwestern France, best known for its world-class wines and elegant 18th-century architecture. The city sits along the Garonne River, where merchants once traded barrels of deep red vintages bound for royal tables across Europe.

Beyond wine, Bordeaux thrives as a cultural center. As it’s lined with cafés, art museums, and tree-shaded boulevards, The Place de la Bourse reflects softly on the Miroir d’Eau, merging history and modern life into a sprawling quayside that locals and travelers alike wander at dusk.
